%- block legend
This is an interactive problem.

The jury has secretly chosen an integer $S$ such that $1 \leq S \leq N$. Your
task is to find $S$ using at most $\VAR{vars.Q.max}$ guesses.
%- endblock

%- block interaction
First, read a single integer $N$ ($\VAR{vars.N.min} \leq N \leq \VAR{vars.N.max}$),
the size of the search range.

Then, you may make at most $\VAR{vars.Q.max}$ guesses. To make a guess, print a
single integer $X$ ($1 \leq X \leq N$) on its own line. After each guess, read
the jury's response, which is one of the following three strings:

\begin{itemize}
	\item \texttt{<} --- meaning $X < S$;
	\item \texttt{>} --- meaning $X > S$;
	\item \texttt{=} --- meaning $X = S$.
\end{itemize}

As soon as you read \texttt{=}, your guess was correct: stop guessing and
terminate your program.

After printing each guess, do not forget to print a newline and flush the
output buffer. Otherwise, you may receive a verdict of
\textit{Time limit exceeded}.
%- endblock
